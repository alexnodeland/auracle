### Changed: on a phone, each level fits the screen, and its buttons are a finger's size

- **Each level fits the phone's screen, like an app's view.** On a phone
  PERFORM, EVOLVE and LEARNING used to run past the bottom of the screen, so
  you scrolled the whole level to reach the controls, and on a phone on its
  side EVOLVE's **PICK** was below the fold. Now the level stays put: the
  pair's faces take the room the words leave so **PLAY** and **PICK** are
  always in sight, PERFORM's well is about a fifth of the screen with its controls
  under it in a column that scrolls on its own, and LEARNING's map sits
  beside its other panels on a phone on its side.
- **Buttons, tabs and fields are at least 44 px on a touch screen.** Many
  were 20 to 34 px: the bank's tabs, search and row buttons, the menu bar's
  ▶ and **MODEL**, EVOLVE's **PLAY**, **PICK**, *circuit* and *patch*, the
  catalog's chips and rows, PERFORM's **XY** and stage buttons, the keys'
  octave and volume, and the levels' stops at the right edge of a tablet.
- **Labels no longer break one word to a line.** EVOLVE's *⇄ circuit* and
  *↓ patch*, PATCH's acts (the last one sat alone on a second line) and
  what a patch is made of (cut short with *…*) read whole.
- **On a phone, toasts appear at the top, under the menu bar.** At the
  bottom of the screen a toast covered the guide's step, PERFORM's controls
  and EVOLVE's picks, and on a phone on its side the levels at the right
  edge. Now it stands over the sound's picture and clear of the levels.
- On a phone, the octave's keys show ‹ and › instead of Z and X, which a
  phone has no keys for.
- **The rack takes a finger's tap where it is drawn small.** On a touch
  screen the ▶ on the face at OUT answers a finger 44 px across, and with a
  module in hand a tap beside a lit socket places it there (the nearest one
  within 22 px), as does a cable let go beside one.
- **UNDER THE HOOD's rows are a finger's size on a tablet**, the hood
  scrolling to the rest.
- **On the narrowest phones (312 px)** every level's name in the bar along
  the bottom is whole (LEARNING was cut), and the sound in hand keeps its
  picture beside its ▶. Short captions that take two lines take two of a
  length, never a word or two left alone.
