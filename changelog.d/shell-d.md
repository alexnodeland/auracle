### Added: ⌘K finds any sound and anything to do

- **Press ⌘K (Ctrl K off a Mac), or Find or do anything in the menu bar,
  and one list opens with everything the instrument can do and every sound
  it holds.** Type part of what you want (a sound's name, a level, *octave*,
  *download*), choose with the arrows, and press Enter. What the level
  you're at does comes first, then what works anywhere (the levels, the
  keys bar and every setting in KEYS ⋯, your files and your taste, booth
  mode, the films), then the sounds, each with its face. Each command shows
  the key that does the same, so the list teaches the keys as you go. A
  sound you choose is in your hands at the level you're at
  (`cmdk.spec.js`, #132).
- **? anywhere but over one of PERFORM's controls opens the same list.**
  Over a control, ? still shows what that control does (`cmdk.spec.js`,
  #132).
- **The guide has a page for the levels:** *One space*, on how PERFORM,
  PATCH, EVOLVE, TASTE and LEARNING fit together, how to move between them,
  the model view, and ⌘K (#132).

### Removed: the ⋯ menu and the ? card

- **The menu bar's ⋯ and ? are gone, and everything ⋯ held is in ⌘K's
  list under the same words:** Download your taste, Open a taste file…,
  Download this patch, Download as a picture…, Open a patch file…, Scope &
  analyzer…, Re-run the three-pick warm start, Reset your taste…, Show
  measurements, Booth mode, New visitor and Watch the films. The ? card's
  key map lives in the guide's keyboard page, which *Keys and gestures* in
  the list opens, and its link to a level's film is *Watch PERFORM in
  depth* (and the others) once that film is out (#132).
