### Changed: the site is set on the instrument's type scale

- **The landing page, the brand page, the 404, the guide, the reference and
  their live figures now take their text sizes, spaces, corners and speeds
  from the same scale as the instrument.** Each page wrote its own before, a
  little apart from the others and from the app. On the landing page, the
  small print in buttons, cards and the footer reads at 16 or 12 px where it
  ran anywhere from 12 to 15, and the headings over Browse, Build and Grow,
  and over the three ways to start, now stand a size above their words. The
  figures' axis labels are set at 11 px, the smallest size a page sets, where
  they were 9 and 10. The AURACLE wordmark in the brand page's bar and on
  the 404 reads at 14 px, where it was 16. Text in the guide and the
  reference moves by a pixel or two at most.
