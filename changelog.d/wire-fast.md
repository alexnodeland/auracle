### Changed: PERFORM's controls play the moment a sound is in your hands

- A sound you haven't played used to keep its controls silent while
  PERFORM measured it: seconds on a fast computer, and a minute or more on
  a slow one for a sound with many knobs. Its controls now turn at once,
  on a wiring PERFORM has without rendering anything: a sound of the same
  shape it has already measured (the same modules and selectors), or else
  what each kind of knob usually does, learned from measuring the presets
  and pools of sounds. That second kind plays only the controls it gets
  right well more often than not (**Bright**, **Snap** and **Space** among
  the six today); the rest say *listening…* until the sound is measured.
  The status line says *listening…* while the sound is measured, naming
  the controls still waiting, and counts the controls that reach the sound
  once it is measured; the controls that play are drawn with a thin, dim
  travel and say *not measured yet* until then. When the measurement lands,
  the controls settle on what the sound really does, without moving what
  you hear, and never while you hold one.
- A sound you have played before no longer re-measures after every pick:
  its controls go stale only when the model's scale moves.
