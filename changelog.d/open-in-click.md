### Fixed: a sound you open plays at the click

- **Opening a sound puts it under the keys the moment you click it.** A row
  in the bank, a card in EVOLVE or TEACH, a sound on TASTE's map, a preset
  and the first of your picks when you press TEACH IT all used to wait for
  the engine to finish whatever it was doing first: a measurement, a face,
  a refit. On a slower laptop that was over a second, and up to twenty
  behind a refit. The app now keeps what the keys need for every sound in
  your bank and every preset, so the sound plays and PERFORM's controls
  work in the same moment as the click, and PATCH's rack follows once the
  engine has drawn it. A preset you had never opened, or had not opened
  since the app was updated, was silent until the engine had loaded it; it
  plays at once too. TASTE's green ring moves to the sound when you click
  it, not when it has finished opening (#306).
