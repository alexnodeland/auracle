### Changed: the launch film shows the instrument as it is now

- **The film on the landing page is new.** It draws PERFORM, EVOLVE and
  PATCH as the app draws them today, with each sound's own face, and cuts
  to the real app three times: turning BRIGHT and letting WANDER drift, an
  offer blended and taken, and the face carried from PERFORM into PATCH
  and out to TASTE and LEARNING, lit by the sound as it goes.
- **It opens on a circuit, not a wall of knobs.** The camera pulls back
  over every preset's real patch, its feedback loops lighting, and the
  cloud that forms the logo rises from their knobs.
- **It shows how the picking works again.** A taste map shows the model's
  guess closing in on your sound as you pick, new patches growing toward
  it, and what BRIGHT and WANDER reach. The map says it is an illustration.
- **It sounds like the instrument.** The bed plays in the films' own key
  and room from the first frame, the narration names the levels it shows,
  and three times the narrator stops and you hear the instrument alone.
