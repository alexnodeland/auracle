### Removed: the names that popped up beside the levels

- **Pointing at the levels at the stage's right edge no longer puts every
  level's name over the stage.** Each name used to show at once, in a pill
  beside its stop, over whatever the level had drawn there, and a stop
  reached with the keyboard showed its own. A stop's name and its key are
  now its tooltip (*Taste · ⌥4*), a screen reader still hears its name and
  what it is (*Taste: the sound among all sounds*), and the menu bar names
  the level you're at (`shell_levels.spec.js`, #286).
