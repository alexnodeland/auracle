### Fixed: a pick you can still take back always counts

- **A pick in its seven seconds is no longer thrown away.** A generation
  ending, or a preset or a patch file you opened, could replace one of the
  two sounds of a pick you could still take back, and when the seven seconds
  were up the pick was refused (*Soft Wash was replaced, so the pick wasn’t
  recorded.*) and **TAUGHT** went back down. Now nothing replaces the two
  sounds of that pick, nor the pair on EVOLVE’s table, until the pick counts,
  you take it back, or the pair is put away. Hovering **EVOLVE POOL** never
  marks them *may be replaced* either.
