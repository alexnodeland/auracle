### Changed: on a phone each level stands still

- **Nothing scrolls on a phone.** Each level is one screen with the keys up
  or down: no level scrolls under your finger, and a swipe up on an EVOLVE
  card always picks it. Only a list scrolls, as a phone's own lists do: the
  bank's, the catalog's, and the rows under the hood.
- **PERFORM and LEARNING show one part at a time on a phone.** Two tabs on
  PERFORM, **controls** and **under the hood**, and three on LEARNING,
  **where liking rises**, **what it weighs** and **its forecasts**, each
  showing its part of the level.
- **The keys take less of the screen.** With them up, the octave, the
  volume, **REC** and **KEYS ⋯** sit on one row over the keybed on every
  phone (MIDI moves inside **KEYS ⋯** there), and
  what a level shows to look at (PERFORM's well, EVOLVE's map and the row
  under the pair) gives its room to what you play until the keys go down.
- **A word on a phone is a banner over the menu bar**, the screen's width,
  on one or two lines with its action beside it, covering nothing in the
  level. Swipe it up to put it away. The model view's tag rides there too.
- **The bar along the bottom of a phone runs PERFORM, PATCH, TASTE,
  LEARNING**, then EVOLVE and KEYS.
- **A bank row on a phone shows its name and its ▶.** Swipe it left to slide
  out its ★, save and cut; swipe it back, scroll the list or tap another row
  and they go.
- What a phone's bar already names is left off the levels there: EVOLVE's
  heading and the levels' small names over their titles.

### Fixed: a long press on a phone selects no text

- **A long press selects nothing.** On an iPhone a long press on an EVOLVE
  card or a bank row selected the words under it and raised the magnifier,
  and on a bank row the model view never came. Nothing in the app is
  selected by a finger now, but in a field.

### Fixed: a key on a phone always sounds

- **A key tapped after the phone was interrupted sounds.** After a call, the
  lock screen or another app's sound, iOS leaves the audio paused in a way
  the app did not wake, and a tapped key made no sound and moved no face.
  It wakes now on the key's press and again on its lift.
- **A key struck after an octave shift plays its note once.** Each shift
  added another copy of the keybed's handling, so a key played its note once
  for every shift made.

### Fixed: a phone's controls drawn true

- **The volume's fader on a phone is lit to its thumb**, on a thin track,
  where a pill the fader's height stood behind it with a fixed glow.
- **Nothing stays lit after a tap** on a touch screen but what is on: a
  tapped button kept its pointer's light.
- **PERFORM's XY and stage buttons hold their marks in their middles,** the
  octave's arrows sit level with the octave, and EVOLVE's **CIRCUIT** and
  **PATCH** hold their words inside their boxes on a phone (the card's
  letter moves to the well's foot).
