An instrument answers the hand. Everything else can wait.

Six reviewers walked every view of Auracle against the evidence we already had: the stills and timing logs of five rehearsed films, the films themselves, and the code. They found 119 things. Most of them come from four causes. This page names the causes, sets the rules that remove them, fixes the shared language, and orders the work.

Diagnosis

Four causes behind most of what's wrong

Sound itself is fast: a knob reaches the voices on the same frame, a duel's ▶ sounds in 30–40 ms, ⌘Z lands in 0.29 s. The trouble starts wherever the app has to think, speak, or name something.

1

One engine thread does everything, and some of its calls take minutes

A generation holds the engine for 103–193 s. ⚡ evolve from this is one 23 s call. A new patch's controls stay dead for 11–16 s. The second Offer takes 10.9 s where the first took 0.01. While a generation runs, a pick waits up to a whole seed (about 19 s) for its next pair, and PERFORM's measurements, offers and drifts wait for all of it.

It says things before they are true, or after they stop being true

A turned knob's readout goes back to the old value. A socket marked EMPTY plays a saw. "● it just learned" appears when the refit is sent, minutes early during a generation. Principles

Eight rules, each with a test

A rule we can't check drifts. Each one below says how we'll know it holds, and most of the tests become browser specs.

The hands are never blocked

A gesture is acknowledged on the thing touched within 100 ms, and gives a first result within 1 s when the work is local. Background work never delays a gesture by more than 250 ms.

TestNo single engine-worker call runs longer than 250 ms. A pick, a ▶ and a bank open are each answered within 1 s during a fit, an offer, ⚡ and a generation.

Say it when it's true

Status appears when the state holds, not when it is requested, and goes away when the state ends. A readout shows what is sounding.

Test"it just learned" follows fitted. A knob readout equals its aria-valuetext 2 s after a turn. An empty socket's peak is below −60 dBFS.

Nothing moves under the hands

Rows, plates, dials, pots and views stay where the player left them. A change the player didn't make is animated so it can be followed, or it waits until the pointer leaves.

TestBank order is frozen while the pointer is in the list. A structural edit keeps the edited plate's neighbour on screen within 4 px.

One idea, one word, one look, one key

The glossary below is the vocabulary. The colour contract has no exceptions. A key does the same thing in every view where it works.

Testwww/checknames.py extends to a banned-words list for UI copy (vote, bench, lens, retired, HELD).

A guess looks like a guess

Anything the model is unsure of is drawn and worded as unsure: hollow, whiskered, "a hunch". Nothing the model says is drawn more certain than it is.

TestNo bar whose interval crosses zero is drawn solid. Map size uses an absolute floor, so six picks can't look as sure as sixty.

Every teaching act can be taken back

A pick, a take, a pass, a star, a cut and a save each get the same 7 s undo, starting when its receipt appears, through the same ⌘Z. An undo says what it undid.

Test⌘Z never changes a patch the player can't see. The sixth pick is undoable.

Long work has one home

Anything over 3 s appears in one place in the menu bar, with progress, time left and stop. It never takes the player to another view.

TestEvery long job posts progress at least once a second and honours stop at its next boundary.

Every view ends in an action

A report invites the next move: hear a maybe, firm it up, hear the children. Every claim about sound can be heard.

TestEach view's empty state and each result names one next step, and it works.

Contract 2

Four colours, four meanings, no exceptions

The stylesheet's law was "green is sound, amber is the model". In the app amber also carried about twenty other jobs. This is the law, completed, with what has to move.

#8ef0b1 · phos-a

Green is sound

Traces, pressed keys, the live row, a playing ▶, dock toggles that change the sound (a lit LED), and the focus ring, always outside the element.

Moves off green: the keyboard cursor row (to silk), a forecast hit (to a word).

#ffb454 · phos-b

Amber is the model

Predictions, belief, forecasts, styles, the picked state (it's what the model hears), search controls (ask the model), and each view's one breed act, ⚡ and EVOLVE POOL, both filled.

Moves off amber: HOLD/UNI/ARP when on, active bank tabs, your own names, locks, alarms, "replaces" sockets, "not measured yet".

#ff5747 · led-red

Red is danger and failure

Destructive on approach (cut, delete, a socket that replaces modules), alarms (a crash, a failed save), recording, below-par.

Gains: the alarm bar, which today is filled amber and looks like an invitation.

#d9d4c8 · silk

Silk is you, and neutral

Saved (filled floppy, rail, the word), your names, your locks (a pin with a hatched halo), the cursor, every active tab.

Up, down, hit and miss become words or glyphs (✓ ✗ ▲ ▼), never colour alone. That is also the colour-blind rule.

Contract 3

Every state visible, and never mistaken for another

Two families of state cause most of the confusion: work that takes time, and claims the model is unsure of.

Work that takes time

Acknowledged · ≤ 100 ms

On the thing touched: the plate dims and names the verb ("unplugging…"), the row shows "opening…", the ▶ gets a dotted ring. Never a toast.

Working · after 1 s

Progress where the player is looking, as a count ("12 / 30"). After 3 s the job also appears in the menu-bar job slot with time left and stop.

Done · when it is

Said on arrival, not on request. A toast only if it is the result of the player's own gesture.

Failed

Says what went wrong and what to do, in red, and stays until handled.

Waiting behind something

If a gesture waits more than 300 ms behind engine work, its control says so: "after the refit…", "waiting for ⚡ (about 15 s)".

What the model claims

Settled

The interval clears zero. Solid bar with its whisker. Words: "fairly sure".

A guess

The interval crosses zero. Hollow bar, full-strength whisker, the label ends in "?". Words: "a hunch", "leaning".

No idea yet

Before the first fit, or too few picks. Says how many picks until it will know: "1 more pick →", "14 to go".

Your verdict beside its guess

Your stars and cuts drawn on the map; "you: ★1 · it still guesses 78% — keep telling it".

Mock · one mark for a pull, in STYLES, DIRECTIONS and PATCH alike

Caption under DIRECTIONS: "Where each style leans. Solid = it's sure. Hollow = still a guess — the thin line is how far it could be off." Above the rows: "12 rows · 1 settled · least settled: loudness — picks between dense and sparse sounds would teach it most."

Contract 4

One undo

  1. ⌘Z first takes back the newest teaching act still inside its window, in any view: a pick, a take, a pass, a star, a cut or a save. It is the one whose receipt is on screen.
  2. Every teaching act gets 7 s, counted from when its receipt becomes visible, not from the press. The observation is held back, not logged and then compensated (keep this).
  3. The sixth pick is undoable too. The refit is sent when its window closes, or when the next pick commits it, and the meter says "● learning from your last 6…" meanwhile.
  4. Edit undo belongs to PATCH. Elsewhere ⌘Z with nothing to take back says "nothing to undo here — PATCH edits undo in PATCH" and changes nothing.
  5. One label: UNDO. Structural edits keep their specific verbs (PUT IT BACK, PLUG IT BACK IN), which say more. When a window closes the button goes away, instead of a dead "IN THE LOG".

The four views

What changes where the player is

The decisions that change how each view behaves. Smaller fixes are in the index at the bottom.

PERFORM

the instrument: named controls, B, Wander
Mock · four control states, each with one look and one meaning

Today the half-closed ring is drawn on the closed side (an SVG dash pattern that assumes a circle starts at 12 o'clock; it starts at 3). And a control that simply hasn't been measured wears the search look, so turning it grafts a module.

Captions say what you can do

"turns toward restless only", not "at the still end", which the amber dot often contradicts. The dot gets a tooltip: where this sound measures, compared with your session.

B's controls in one place, in the order you use them

Pads grouped [Keep Back] · [Offer Peek Take] · [Freeze], Freeze under Wander. Blend leaves the timbre deck and becomes the B strip's crossfader, home ◀━●━▶ B.

The second offer is as fast as the first

A spare grows while B is occupied. Offer becomes NEXT · passes on B while B holds one, a pass gets UNDO, and an unheard pass says it wasn't counted. After a pass Blend comes home, as it does after a Take.

"Heard" is visible

B's tag fills as B is heard and shows B ✓ at one second, when a Take or Next starts to count.

Wander answers at once

The first move comes 1.5 s after the hand leaves, not 21 s. Wander's caption carries its own state ("drift · next in 9 s", "paused 3 s", "held"), with a countdown arc and zone ticks on the ring.

Controls stay under the hands

Re-centring glides over 250 ms with a ghost tick; a MIDI pot re-anchors instead of letting go. Background re-checks never re-centre unless the wiring changed. A graft never turns the control for you 15 s later.

PATCH

the rack

Readouts tell the truth

A turned knob keeps its value. PERFORM's base follows the hand, and a ghost appears only when PERFORM really holds an offset. An empty socket is silent: the grammar's Silence becomes placeable and is what an unplugged source leaves.

Bypass is a latch, in place

A BYP button in each plate's well, and B on a focused plate. The plate stays at 45% with a straight cable drawn across it and the module crossfades out in 10 ms. No restructuring, no moving screen.

SET ASIDE works like the catalogue

Click or Enter arms a set-aside module, sockets light, Enter places. It lives at the top of the node-bank rail, so it never takes the rack's height.

Keep as new keeps it

The edit becomes Glass Pad ✎, saved in MY PATCHES, the original untouched: "Kept as Glass Pad ✎ in MY PATCHES — Glass Pad is unchanged."

The comparison is blind and quick

Sides labelled A and B, revealed after the pick. Space flips at the playhead (about 3 s per comparison, not 10). Esc cancels. "…and mine is better" is a one-shot choice, not a sticky checkbox.

The rack holds still, and says so

A structural edit keeps the zoom and the neighbour's place. The plate dims and names the verb within a frame. The model's guess reads 39% with its reasons in words; "was" only when the digits change.

The next pair is already waiting

One pair is dealt ahead with both sounds rendered, so a pick or another pair swaps in at 0 ms, even mid-generation. Until then another pair goes inert like a pick does, and says why.

"It just learned" when it has

"● learning from your last 6…" while the fit runs; "● it just learned — see what changed ▸" when fitted arrives, staying until the next pick.

Listening costs less

↑ flips sides at the same playhead; a playhead and segment ticks under both scopes; an optional auto-listen that plays A then B on every deal. Target: a considered pick in 6 s.

The pool shows what's safe

Saved rows read SAVED; hovering EVOLVE POOL marks the ten that would be replaced. A cut patch is never dealt again. Replacement happens at the end of a generation, so a save made during one protects.

TASTE

what it thinks you like

Styles hold still

Named only from settled pulls, else by ear: "like Warm Wash". A name changes only after a different top pair has held for two refits. New styles are announced once: "A 4th style split off — like Warm Stab ▶".

The chips are the key

Five shades of amber can't tell styles apart. Clicking a chip focuses that style in every tab; the map draws one amber with glow as its only gradient.

The map says how sure

Size gets an absolute floor, so early maps are mostly big, as the guide says. Each dot's tooltip adds a word and a verdict: "93% · a hunch · maybe — worth a listen".

It invites

Hear a maybe ▸ (and N) opens the brightest unsure dot, cycling. Your stars sit on the map beside its guesses.

A refit you can follow

"see what changed" always opens MAP; dots tween by id over 400 ms; the ones that moved 5 points or more are ringed and labelled; one line says what changed.

TRUST in words

"Its guesses beat a coin flip by 27%." "When it was about 70% sure, you agreed 8 times in 10." Then Firm it up — 10 more picks ▸. The method stays exactly as it is.

Everywhere

menu bar, bank, dock, first run

A row click stays in the view

It plays the patch where you are. Opening it in PATCH is explicit: "↗ patch" on the hovered row, or a double-click.

The bank holds still

Order freezes while the pointer is in the list and for 1.5 s after a click; percentages update in place with ▲/▼; the re-sort animates when you leave. The live row stays in view.

A first minute with no dead controls

PERFORM is the page's first view, not PATCH behind a dialog. The 62 presets ship pre-measured, so a warm-start pick is playable the moment it lands.

Menus and help by kind

⋯ groups Files, Tools, Modes (with ✓), Start over, Help. Help opens on the current view's tab. Booth mode hides the exits behind a staff gesture.

Holding the line

Measured, so it stays fixed

  • Timing marks in the app (performance.mark) at boot start, veil down, first sound, pool full, PERFORM wired, patch opened. The film recorder writes them into every rehearsal sidecar, so each film rehearsal is also a performance run.
  • Budget specs on the CI machine: playable ≤ 5 s with a warm cache; a revisited patch wired ≤ 0.5 s (≤ 1.5 s after reload; today's spec allows 5 s and 8 s); a pick deals ≤ 0.3 s; duel ▶ ≤ 0.15 s; a gesture during any long job answered ≤ 1 s.
  • Truth specs for each Wave 0 fix: knob readout equals its value; an empty socket is silent; "it just learned" never precedes fitted; ⌘Z never changes an unseen patch.
  • A banned-words check in make dev-check for UI copy and the guide: vote, bench, workbench, lens, retired, evicted, HELD, check duel.
Keep

What already works, and must survive the fixes

  • Sound first. Knobs write to the voices before the engine hears of it; swaps are click-free; the ordered bench lane with coalescing.
  • Instant where it's cached: spare offers (0.01 s), revisited wirings (0.12 s), the deal before the fit (17 ms), prefetched duel renders (30–40 ms).
  • The held vote: an undo window that holds the observation back rather than logging and compensating.
  • The toast lane's rules: one lane, replace keys, urgent refusals, windows that start when visible.
  • Hands on always wins over Wander, and nothing in PERFORM is modal.
  • The rack reads as hardware: units on every knob, green audio and amber modulation cables, arm-and-place with named sockets, wrap-onto-occupied.
  • TRUST's method: out-of-sample forecasts, the separate fair-test line, Wilson whiskers. Change its words, never its maths.
  • Honest refusals: "Nothing to breed toward yet", disabled pads that say what they wait for, search controls that say they're asking.
  • The map keeps its orientation, and taste-geom.js stays pure and tested.
  • Gates that destroy nothing, the MIDI indicator's four states, the film chip's once-per-view rule.
