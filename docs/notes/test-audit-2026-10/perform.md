# Test-quality audit: PERFORM's browser specs

Area: `tests/web/perform_*.spec.js` (16 files), `tests/web/pad_keys.spec.js`,
`tests/web/explain.spec.js`, and the helper `tests/web/perform_budget.js`, in
the main checkout at `42bd322`. I read them against `apps/web/perform.js`,
`apps/web/explain.js`, `apps/web/worker.js`, the fixture, the unit tests in
`apps/web/tests`, the Rust tests in `crates/auracle-session` and
`crates/auracle-wasm`, and the guide `www/docs/src/views/perform.md`. Line
numbers are the current checkout's. Test-seconds are the mean of the CI runs
in the blob reports of CI runs 37383132211, 37379867024 and 37345520856 (fast tier) and 37381447489 (slow tier) (three fast-tier runs, one slow-tier run).

The area has 71 tests and about 1,771 test-seconds: about 732 s in the fast
tier and about 1,039 s in the slow tier (`perform_circuit` was in the slow run
as `@quarantine`; #172 put it back in the gate). Every spec is on the fixture.

Severity follows the rubric: flimsy and vacuous findings are high, with two
exceptions marked *medium* where the vacuous line backs no clause of the
title, only a comment. Where a high finding is unlikely to fire, the evidence
says so.

### The worker-protocol harness: does the level exist, and could it?

It does not exist yet. Two partial precedents do:
- `tests/web/boot_agrees.spec.js` already runs the built wasm under Node, opening no page (`engine.initSync({ module: fs.readFileSync(wasm) })`).
- `apps/web/tests/worker-lanes.test.mjs` and `worker-perform-replies.test.mjs` lift single functions out of worker.js's source and run them against stubs.

A full harness is feasible, at moderate effort:
- **The engine:** worker.js's `init` already accepts a precompiled module in the message (`module_or_path: m.module || new URL(…)`), so the harness compiles `apps/web/pkg/auracle_wasm_bg.wasm` once and passes it in.
- **What the harness must supply:**
  - a `self` with `location.href` (a file URL) and `postMessage`/`onmessage`;
  - the glue's dynamic `import("./pkg/auracle_wasm.js?v=…")`, which Node ESM resolves from a file URL with a query;
  - a `fetch` shim for `perform-wirings.json` (worker.js:1969);
  - an IndexedDB stub, or `fake-indexeddb`, for the face store;
  - no answer to `farm_want`, which is the `?farm=0` path.
- **Tests that would move:**
  - both perform_offer_latency tests, 93 s of the slow tier, of which the Keep check (about 25 s) stays in the browser;
  - `measureStep` itself;
  - the engine-only halves of perform_truth's crash tests, which today inject replies by hand.

## Patterns worth a helper, a lint rule or a rule in tests/web/AGENTS.md

1. **PERFORM has no page helper.** These are copied across files:
   - `drag` in perform_aimed, perform_offer_latency, perform_recentre, perform_truth and pad_keys, plus inline in perform_controls, perform_palette and perform_circuit;
   - `peek` with `HEARD_MS` (1_600 in one file, 1_800 in four);
   - `grow`;
   - the in-page `pressOffer` that dispatches PointerEvents;
   - the wired `live()` predicate (four times, plus `fixtures.reached`);
   - `row()` for the palette (three times);
   - the shipped-file abort route;
   - the status and Wander MutationObserver recorders;
   - the output analyser INIT (also in space_after_a_click).

   Put them in `tests/web/perform_page.js`, beside `patch_page.js`.
2. **Speed asserted as wall-clock time in-page** (perform_instant, perform_next), even where the tap already proves the behaviour ("handed over, not grown"; "wired with the engine held"). Rule: a speed promise lives in budgets.spec.js or is relative to a measured step; elsewhere, assert the mechanism.
3. **Transient engine states raced instead of pinned.** Examples: rAF watchers (perform_palette), one-shot reads (perform_truth:146), an ask that may come after the measurement (explain:182), a cancel that may never happen (explain:513), a re-check that may not have started (perform_recentre:88). Rule: hold the request (`app.holdRequests`, `app.stall`, `slowEngine`) for as long as the assertion needs the state.
4. **"Nothing happened" read at once**, for something that would only arrive after an engine reply: `not.toContainText` on `#toasts` straight after the gesture (perform_open_early:96, perform_truth:250, pad_keys:98). Rule: when the wrong outcome goes through the engine, assert through the tap (`app.sent(...)` is empty, held requests included), after `app.quiet()`.
5. **Assertions in a loop or branch that may not run** (perform_truth:313, perform_recentre:91, perform_aimed:83). A lint rule could flag `for…of` or `if` whose body is only `expect`, with no length assertion before it.
6. **Real offers grown to test page state machines.** B's states, pass and undo windows, Blend, the pot, and the aimed strip each grow a real offer, up to 240 s per budget on CI. Rule: answer `perform_offer` from a recorded offer (`app.answer`) unless the engine's offer is the subject. Keep one real end-to-end offer (perform_offer_moments:159).
7. **Web-first idioms.** `expect(await …)`, `page.waitForFunction`, `page.waitForSelector`, `document.activeElement` checks, `#toasts` DOM reads instead of `app.toast`, and 2-5 s `timeout:` overrides on UI states. These fit eslint-plugin-playwright's `prefer-web-first-assertions`, `no-wait-for-selector`, `no-wait-for-timeout` (with `app.quiet` and named pacing constants allowed) and `prefer-to-be-focused`-style checks.
8. **Tagging drifts.** perform_palette:61 averages 64 s on CI and is untagged; perform_aimed reached 42.9 s. shard.mjs has the timings, so a CI step could warn when an untagged test passes 40 s on main.
