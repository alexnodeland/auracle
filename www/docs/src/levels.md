# One space: the levels

<p class="lede">PERFORM, PATCH, EVOLVE, TASTE and LEARNING are not pages. They
are levels of one space around the sound you’re playing: zoom out to hear it
among every sound, zoom in to see what it is made of, and step beside it to
breed what it could become. ⌘K finds anything in any of them.</p>

## Where each level is

The menu bar names the level you’re at, with its line beside it:

| Level | Where it is | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [LEARNING](./views/learning.md) | Past TASTE, out at the top | How it learns your taste |
| [TASTE](./views/taste.md) | Zoomed out | The sound among all sounds |
| [PERFORM](./views/perform.md) | In the middle, where the app opens | The sound, under your hands |
| [PATCH](./views/play.md) | Zoomed in | What the sound is made of |
| [EVOLVE](./views/evolve.md) | Beside PERFORM | What it could become |

The levels at the right edge of the stage draw this as a cross: a stop for
each level, out at the top, in at the bottom, and EVOLVE’s stop branching
left of PERFORM’s. The stop you’re at is lit. The app opens at PERFORM, or at
the level you were at last time, and a link that ends in a level’s name
(`…/play/#taste`) opens that level.

## Move between them

1. Click a stop on the levels.
2. Or press <kbd>⌥↑</kbd> to zoom out and <kbd>⌥↓</kbd> to zoom in,
   <kbd>⌥←</kbd> to go beside it to EVOLVE and <kbd>⌥→</kbd> to come back.
   From EVOLVE, out goes to TASTE and in to PATCH.
3. Or press <kbd>⌥1</kbd> to <kbd>⌥5</kbd> to go straight to PERFORM, PATCH,
   EVOLVE, TASTE or LEARNING (Alt off Apple platforms).
4. Or hold <kbd>⌥</kbd> and turn the wheel, one level a turn; a trackpad’s
   pinch does the same, except over PATCH’s patch, which it zooms instead.
5. On a touch screen, spread two fingers to zoom in and pinch to zoom out.

As you move, the face of the sound you’re playing flies from where one level
draws it to where the next does: PERFORM’s well, the face at **OUT** in
PATCH, its mark on TASTE’s map, its ring on LEARNING’s, and the card in
EVOLVE that holds it, if one does. Where a level doesn’t draw it, the face
fades instead of flying. A light travels along the levels from the stop you
left to the one you reach, and at either end of the zoom the levels nod and
nothing moves. With reduced motion set on your system every move is
instant.

The keys work with a knob or a list focused, but not in a text field, where
<kbd>⌥←</kbd> moves by a word, nor under a dialog that asks you something.
Every key and gesture is in [the keyboard map](./keyboard.md#the-levels).

## The model view

Hold <kbd>⌥</kbd> for a moment, at any level, and what the model believes
comes up over it: each bank row’s guess and the pool in the model’s order,
TASTE’s halos, EVOLVE’s guess before you pick, PATCH’s belief line and which
way your taste leans on each module. Let go and it goes. **MODEL** in the menu bar does the
same held, and a tap on it keeps the view up until you tap it again or press
<kbd>Esc</kbd>. [Reading what it learned](./reading-the-model.md#the-model-view)
says what each mark means.

## ⌘K: find anything

<kbd>⌘K</kbd> (Ctrl K off Apple platforms), or **Find or do anything** in the
menu bar, opens one list of everything the instrument can do and every sound
it holds. So does <kbd>?</kbd>, anywhere but over one of PERFORM’s controls,
where <kbd>?</kbd> shows what that control does instead.

1. Type part of what you want: a sound’s name, a level, or what to do
   (*taste*, *octave*, *download*).
2. Choose with <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd>, and press <kbd>↵</kbd> to do
   it, or click it.
3. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the list and puts the focus back where it was.

It lists, in three groups:

- **THIS LEVEL:** what the level you’re at does, its pads and buttons: at
  PERFORM, OFFER, TAKE, PASS, KEEP, BACK, ARRANGE, stage mode and *What does
  BRIGHT do?*; at PATCH, ADD MODULE, the camera, the layouts, the locks and
  NEW PATCH; at EVOLVE, PLAY and PICK for each card and ANOTHER PAIR.
- **ANYWHERE:** the levels; play the sound you’re playing; save or cut the
  sound you’re on; the bank’s previous and next; <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>; the model view; the keys
  bar (the octave, VOL, MIDI, ● REC and every setting in **KEYS ⋯**); your
  files ([your data](./your-data.md)); the scope; the warm start again;
  Reset your taste; Show measurements; booth mode; *What are the three
  banks?*; *Learn: what a filter does*; **Record again** for each sound kept
  safe; and links to the films and to this guide’s keys and gestures.
- **SOUNDS:** the pool, then the presets, each with its face. With nothing
  typed it shows five; with a query, the eight that match best. Choosing one
  puts it in your hands at the level you’re at, as <kbd>Enter</kbd> on its
  row in the bank does.

Each command prints its key beside it, where it has one, so the list teaches
the keys as you use it, and a setting that is on says *on*. A name’s start
ranks first, then a word’s start, then the letters anywhere, then the letters
in order with others between: *dyt* finds *Download your taste*.

While the list is open, what you type goes into it: no note plays, no level
moves, and <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> takes nothing back. It is drawn from what the page
already holds, so it opens and narrows at once: a sound’s face is the one
the bank has drawn, and a sound whose face it hasn’t drawn yet keeps an
empty place for it.
