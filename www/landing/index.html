The instrument

One instrument, four views.

PERFORM. Play the sound with controls named for what you hear, from BRIGHT to SPACE. Each one turns the few knobs that move this sound most in its direction. Press OFFER, and it grows a variant into B for you to blend or take.

PATCH. Open a sound to see its patch: every module, cable, and knob, each knob in its own units. Change any of it while you play. Lock what you love, and ⚡ EVOLVE FROM THIS grows new sounds around it.

EVOLVE. Pick between two sounds, and a meter counts the picks until it next redraws your taste. Press EVOLVE POOL, and the log says what each generation changed, knob by knob and module by module.

TASTE. Your sounds on one map, placed by how they sound and how they’re built. A brighter dot is one it rates higher for you; a bigger dot is one it’s less sure about.

How it learns

It can be wrong, and it tells you when.

Every pick tells it which of two sounds you’d reach for. From those picks it learns which way your taste leans, and it can hold more than one way at once: you can love dark drones and bright plucks without it averaging them into neither. Before each pick it guesses yours, and afterward it shows you whether it was right.

How it breeds

Every generation grows a little closer.

Once it has a first guess at your taste, press EVOLVE POOL. The sounds it rates highest each take a short walk: a few knobs move, and now and then a module changes. Each walk ends in a new sound, which joins the pool only if it rates above the weakest one there. The lowest-rated sounds are replaced. A sound you saved never is.

The modules

Every sound is a real instrument.

Not samples. Each sound is a modular patch, built and wired from scratch, and you play it from your computer keys, a MIDI keyboard, or the keys on screen. Open it, and every module, cable, and knob is there to change.

  1. sourcesVCO, supersaw, a wavetable, a physically modeled pluck, a formant oscillator that speaks vowels, and noise
  2. shapeWavefolder, distortion in three drive modes, and bitcrush
  3. filterA filter with three state-variable responses and a diode ladder, and a three-band EQ
  4. spaceDelay, chorus, reverb, phaser, flanger, and granular
  5. motionTremolo, vibrato, and pitch shift
  6. dynamicsCompressor, ducker, and gate, each with a control input you can sidechain into
  7. combineMix, ring mod, and vocoder, each merging two chains into one
  8. modulationLFO, envelope, sample-and-hold, follower, Euclidean rhythm, and a step sequencer
  9. shape cvQuantize, slew, rectify, and hold, which bend a modulator on its way to a knob. They chain: s&h rand → quantize → slew before it ever reaches a cutoff
  10. combine cvSix ways to merge two modulators into one cable

Turn a knob, and you hear it at once. Lock it, and ⚡ EVOLVE FROM THIS leaves it as it is.
