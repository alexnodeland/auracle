# Films

The pipeline is described in `docs/architecture/films.md`; the rules are in
`www/video/AGENTS.md`; every tool is in `www/video/README.md`. This is the
order of work.

## A walkthrough, from nothing to published

1. **Script** `films/<name>/script.json`: beats and lines. Write what the app
   does, in its own words; plan each claim so the recording can show it.
   Keep a `storyboard.md` beside it: what each beat shows.
2. **Voice**: `make film-voice FILM=<name>`. The ASR round trip must read
   every line back; fix pronunciation in `films/lexicon.json` (one
   read-modify-write, it is shared). Then run the film's timing script (tails)
   if it has one.
3. **Shots** `shots.json`, usually generated by a `gen_shots.py` that imports
   `www/video/tools/shotgen.py` for the seeded session, waits and dumps. Time
   actions to words (`at`, `until`) and to app states (stamps); use `clips` to
   cut waits the viewer should not sit through.
4. **Check**: `node www/video/tools/validate.mjs <name>`.
5. **Rehearse**: `make film-rehearse FILM=<name>` (runs through the browser
   queue). Read each shot's sidecar in `out/<name>/dry/`. Every shot must pass
   with no errors and no late actions. `python3 www/video/tools/framing.py <name>`
   for callouts and crops.
6. **Truth**: every on-screen claim true of the recorded session (the
   `truth-pass` skill). When the app falls short, fix or report the app.
7. **Record and render** on a quiet machine:
   `make film-record FILM=<name> POSTER=<seconds>`. One film at a time; watch
   disk (`docs/runbooks/disk-full.md`).
8. **Review** before publishing: `python3 www/video/tools/takes.py <name>`
   clean; a contact sheet of frames; integrated loudness about −16 LUFS; the
   poster. Send a 720p MP4 preview for human review (uploads stop at 30 MB).
9. **Publish**: add chapter names (`CHAPTER_NAMES`) and the silent loop
   (`VIEW_LOOPS`) to `www/video/tools/publish.py`, then
   `make film-publish FILMS="<name>"`, then `make site && make site-check`.

## An illustrated film

Script and voice as above; the picture is `film.js` drawing beats on the
stage; render with `make film FILM=<name> POSTER=<seconds>`.

## When the app changes under a film

Rehearse the affected shots again. A changed label, toast or number may make
a line or callout false: fix the film's text (and re-voice), or fix the app.
The `film-producer` agent can take a film end to end to a clean rehearsal.
