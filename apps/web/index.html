    HOW TO PLAY
    perform: play it

    Bright, Snap, Motion, Body, Grit, and Space are named for what you hear, and Arrange places up to eight of the palette’s eighteen, in your order. Each is wired to the knobs of this sound that do that job, measured when it opens, and the strip underneath shows them move. An amber control is one this sound’s knobs can’t reach: turn it anyway, and it adds what is missing or grows a variant that can. A control that says listening… hasn’t been measured on this sound yet, and does nothing until it has.

    Offer (n) grows a new version of what you are playing into B, beside it in the well. Hold Peek (or b) to hear it, slide Blend under the two to move between them, and Take (⇧↵) to make it yours. While B holds one, the pad reads Next: it passes on B and brings the next; Pass passes without another. Once you have heard an offer, either answer teaches the model, and either can be taken back from its note.

    Wander, at the start of the pads, lets the model play along: a little brings ideas into B, more drifts the knobs toward your taste, and all the way roams. Let go of it and it answers in a moment, and the line under it says what it is doing; tap it to freeze it. When the sound has moved from where you last kept it, moved · keep · back shows by its name: Keep (↵, with nothing focused) marks where you are and Back (⇧⌫) returns there. XY in the well’s corner puts two controls under one finger, Arrange chooses what your velocity plays, and a MIDI controller’s first eight knobs claim the first eight controls.

    the levels

    The levels at the right edge say where you are, and a click on one goes there. ⌥↑ zooms out (PATCH, PERFORM, TASTE, LEARNING) and ⌥↓ in · ⌥← goes beside it to EVOLVE and ⌥→ back · ⌥1–5 go straight to PERFORM, PATCH, EVOLVE, TASTE or LEARNING · hold ⌥ (or model) for the model view: what it believes, over any level; a tap on model keeps it, Esc ends it

    keys

    a w s e d f t g y h u j k play notes · z/x octave · ⌘Z take back a pick or cut (7 s), else in PATCH undo an edit · ⇧⌘Z redo

    Space hear the sound you’re playing · in PERFORM, n offer, hold b to peek, ⇧↵ take, ↵ keep, ⇧⌫ back, ⇧F stage mode, and ? on a control shows what it does to this sound, measured (BRIGHT’s has a lesson on filters) · [/] step through the bank · 1–5 rate the sound you’re on · m save it · in the bank, c compares a bred sound with its seed · in presets, p plays the row

    In EVOLVE: 1/2 hear A or B · ←/→ pick · n another pair · ⌘Z take back a pick (7 s, the sixth too)

    the patch's canvas

    ⇧Home fit the whole patch · . fit what you’re on · ⌘0 actual size · ⌘−/⌘= zoom out/in · the same in the well’s corner: fit, −, +

    Ctrl and the wheel, or a pinch, zooms at the pointer · the wheel or a drag on bare canvas pans · Space and a drag, or a middle-button drag, pans from anywhere on it. map shows where you are (Shift-click it to bookmark a spot, ⇧1–9 to go back); at the edges, ‹ and › say how many modules lie past them.

    The layout’s ▾: chain draws the signal path on one baseline, compact packs it tight, and by hand is yours: drag a module by its face and it snaps to the grid, or hold Shift to place it freely. snap to the grid pins everything where it sits now, which is how you start from a bred patch. Positions are kept per patch, survive a reload and a ⚡ generation, and travel with a downloaded patch file. detail leaves each module three knobs to read when you zoom out.

    the keybed

    vol, midi and ● rec sit at the dock's right end: ● rec bounces your playing to a WAV. keys ⋯ holds the rest, and is lit while any of hold, uni, arp or sync is on: hold latches notes · arp plays held chords in patterns at the BPM, with octave range, gate, and swing · uni stacks all 4 voices detuned · glide slides one note into the next when you play a line (chords stay clean) · ◼ silence stops every voice

    ⇕ tall gives the keybed the height of a real keyboard, and span sets how many octaves it shows: fewer octaves, wider keys. Both are for playing with your fingers.

    A MIDI keyboard plays it, with velocity, pitch bend, and the sustain pedal.

    the patch

    Drag a knob and the sound changes at once. Click a named setting (a wave, a filter mode) to cycle it. The selected module shows ⋯, its structure menu (or right-click it, or F2), and ▢, which locks it so breeding can’t touch it (l). Tab into the patch, then ←/→ walk the modules in signal order, ↑/↓ go into a module’s modulators and back, Home/End the first and the last, Enter into its knobs: ↑/↓ turn (Shift finely), Space or Enter cycles a setting (Shift goes back), Esc back out. A click on the face at OUT plays the sound.

    the catalog

    add module (or /) opens the catalog over the well. Click a module and it’s in your hand: every socket it can go into lights up and says what will happen there. Green inserts it after what’s already in the socket, and amber replaces it. Click a lit ○ to place it, or ▶ on the line along the well’s top to hear it there first. Esc puts it down, and closes the catalog.

    / searches by sound as well as by name: grit, vowel, sidechain, wander. Point at any module for a sentence on what it does and its ports (hold ⌥ to see which way your taste leans on it). Arrows walk the list; Enter takes one; then arrows walk the lit sockets and Enter places.

    Modulation chains. Drop a shape cv module (quantize, slew, rectify, hold) on a cable that already carries a modulator and it takes that modulator as its input instead of replacing it, so s&h rand → quantize → slew is three clicks. combine cv takes two modulators and gives back one. The socket says which of fill, replace, or shape it is about to do.

    Drag a wired in jack off its socket to unplug: the chain is set aside, even across a reload. set aside at the well’s foot opens the shelf (the catalog lists it first too), and dragging its ● onto a ○ puts it back.

    teaching it

    Pick between pairs in EVOLVE (or teach at the foot of PATCH’s well). Star and cut sounds in the bank: point at a row for its actions. keep as new plays your edit against the original and asks which you’d reach for (tick “pick the edit” to skip that, once). TASTE draws each pick as it learns from it, and LEARNING shows what it weighs: name your styles there. The ? beside Find a sound walks you through what a generation is.

    The rail holds three banks. pool is the 40 sounds the model weighs and breeds from: a bred sound that joins it replaces the lowest-rated one it can (not saved, nor just kept as new). presets is the hand-made library that came with the instrument. saved is what you saved.

    ★ and save are different questions. Stars tell the model what you think of a sound; save tells the bank to keep it, and no generation replaces a saved sound. Press m on the row the bank cursor is on to save it.
