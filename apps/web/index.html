HOW TO PLAY
keys

a w s e d f t g y h u j k … play notes · z/x octave · ⌘Z undo edit · ⇧⌘Z redo

In EVOLVE: 1/2 audition · ←/→ vote

performance

hold latches notes · arp plays held chords in patterns at the BPM · uni stacks all 4 voices detuned · gld is portamento · ● rec bounces your playing to a WAV

A MIDI keyboard just works: velocity, pitch bend, sustain pedal.

the rack

Drag knobs — sound changes instantly. Click enum plates to cycle. ⋯ restructures a module; ▢ locks it so evolution can't touch it.

Stage modules from the node bank, then drag the tray jack onto a matching in/mod jack. Drag a wired in jack off to unplug into the tray.

teaching it

Vote duels in EVOLVE (or the quick-duel strip in PLAY). Star and cut patches in the bank. Commit edits with “my edit is better”. Watch TASTE learn your styles — name them.

Everything autosaves. patch ⤓ shares one patch as a file.
