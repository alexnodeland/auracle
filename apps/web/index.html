HOW TO PLAY
keys

a w s e d f t g y h u j k … play notes · z/x octave · ⌘Z undo edit · ⇧⌘Z redo

space audition the current patch · [/] step through the bank · 1–5 rate the patch you’re on · m save it · in presets, p plays the row

In EVOLVE: 1/2 audition · ←/→ vote · ⌘Z take back a vote

the rack canvas

home fit the whole patch · . fit what you’re on · ⌘0 actual size · ⌘−/⌘= zoom out/in

ctrl+wheel or pinch zooms at the pointer · wheel or a drag on bare canvas pans · space-drag and middle-drag pan from anywhere on it. map shows where you are; detail drops the knobs when you zoom out.

chain draws the signal path on one baseline, compact packs it tight, and freeform is yours: drag a plate by its faceplate and it snaps to the grid — hold shift to place it freely. apply grid pins everything where it currently sits, which is how you start from an evolved patch. Positions are kept per patch, survive a reload and a generation of ⚡, and travel with an exported patch file.

performance

hold latches notes · arp plays held chords in patterns at the BPM, with octave range, gate and swing · uni stacks all 4 voices detuned · gld slides one note into the next when you play a line (chords stay clean) · ● rec bounces your playing to a WAV

⇕ tall gives the keybed the height of a real keyboard, and keys sets how many octaves it shows — fewer octaves, wider keys. Both are worth having if you're playing with fingers.

A MIDI keyboard just works: velocity, pitch bend, sustain pedal.

the rack

Drag knobs — sound changes instantly. Click enum plates to cycle. ⋯ restructures a module; ▢ locks it so evolution can't touch it. Tab into the rack, then arrows to move and ↑/↓ to turn.

the node bank

The rail on the right is the catalogue. Click a module and it's in your hand: every socket it can go into lights up and says what will happen there — green inserts before what's already in the socket, amber replaces it. Click a lit ○ to place it. esc puts it down.

/ searches by sound as well as by name — grit, vowel, sidechain, wander. Hover any module for a sentence on what it does, its ports, and what the model has learned about it. Arrows walk the list; enter takes one; then arrows walk the lit sockets and enter places.

Modulation chains. Drop a shape cv module — quantize, slew, rectify, hold — on a cable that already carries a modulator and it takes that modulator as its input instead of replacing it, so s&h rand → quantize → slew is three clicks. combine cv takes two modulators and gives back one. The socket says which of fill / replace / shape it is about to do.

Drag a wired in jack off its socket to unplug — the chain is held under the rack until you reload, and dragging its ● puts it back.

teaching it

Pick between pairs in EVOLVE (or the quick-pick strip in PLAY). Star and cut patches in the bank. Commit edits with “my edit is better”. Watch TASTE learn your styles — name them. The bank's ? walks you through what a generation is.

The rail holds three banks. evolution is the live pool of 40: each bred patch replaces the one the model thinks you like least. presets is the hand-made library that came with the instrument. my patches is what you saved.

★ and save are different questions. Stars tell the model what you think of a patch; save tells the bank to keep it — a saved patch is never replaced to make room. Press m on the row the bank cursor is on to save it.
